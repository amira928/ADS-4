TASK 4: Shortest Path from Edinburgh to Dundee

Road network (miles):
- Edinburgh to Stirling: 50
- Edinburgh to Glasgow: 70
- Edinburgh to Perth: 100
- Glasgow to Stirling: 50
- Stirling to Perth: 40
- Perth to Dundee: 60

Dijkstra's algorithm:

1. Start at Edinburgh (0)
2. Update neighbors: Stirling=50, Glasgow=70, Perth=100
3. Pick Stirling (50), update Perth to 90 (50+40)
4. Pick Glasgow (70), no updates
5. Pick Perth (90), update Dundee to 150 (90+60)
6. Pick Dundee (150) - done!

Result: Edinburgh → Stirling → Perth → Dundee = 150 miles
