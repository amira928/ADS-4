Task 2 – Breadth First Search (BFS)

Adjacency Lists:
A: C B D
B: A C E G
C: A B D
D: C A
E: G F B
F: G E
G: F B

Source vertex: A

Detailed Trace of BFS:

Queue:
A

Removes A from the queue and puts its adjacent vertices
C, B, and D on the queue, marking each of them.

Queue:
C B D

Removes C from the queue and checks its adjacent vertices
A, B, and D, which are already marked.

Queue:
B D

Removes B from the queue and checks its adjacent vertices
A and C, which are already marked.
Puts adjacent vertices E and G on the queue,
marking each of them.

Queue:
D E G

Removes D from the queue and checks its adjacent vertices
C and A, which are already marked.

Queue:
E G

Removes E from the queue and checks its adjacent vertices.
G is already marked.
Puts adjacent vertex F on the queue and marks it.
B is already marked.

Queue:
G F

Removes G from the queue and checks its adjacent vertices
F and B, which are already marked.

Queue:
F

Removes F from the queue and checks its adjacent vertices
G and E, which are already marked.

Final BFS Order:
A → C → B → D → E → G → F
